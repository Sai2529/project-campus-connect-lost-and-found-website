Lost Something on Campus?
Let's Reconnect It Safely.

Misplacing calculators, drawing sets, ID cards, or notebooks during lab classes and exams shouldn't mean losing them forever. CampusConnect brings transparency, smart matching, and secure ownership verification to the GKM campus community.

The Problem

Lost items in workshop halls, Turing computer labs, and bus bays often remain unclaimed or get scattered across unofficial WhatsApp groups without privacy.

The Solution

One central digital portal with keyword search, smart similarity matching, hidden secret verification, and official return logging.

How CampusConnect Keeps Claims Safe

Four simple steps designed to guarantee only genuine owners recover their belongings.

01

Report Belonging

Student logs details, location, and sets a private ownership question.

02

Smart Match Alerts

System cross-checks records and flags matching lost and found listings.

03

Submit Proof

Claimant provides their student registration number and hidden proof detail.

04

Verified Handover

Proof is verified and the report is officially updated to 'Returned'.
